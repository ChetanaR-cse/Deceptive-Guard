## 🚨 The Problem
E-commerce dark patterns—such as pre-checked subscription boxes, hidden service fees, fake urgency timers, and confusing monetary formatting—cost consumers billions of dollars annually. Everyday shoppers lack the legal and technical expertise to audit malicious checkout interfaces before paying.

## 💡 The Solution: Deceptive-Guard
**Deceptive-Guard** is an advanced multimodal AI forensic agent powered by **Google Gemma**. By uploading a screenshot of any shopping cart or checkout screen, users instantly receive:
1. **Scam Risk Score** (% risk assessment with critical alerts).
2. **Deceptive Pattern Breakdown** (plain-language explanation of hidden tricks).
3. **Policy & Law Violations** (citations of FTC unfair trade rules and e-commerce laws).
4. **Instant Consumer Complaint Report** (ready-to-use text to report the merchant).

---

## ⚡ Key Features
- **Multimodal Vision Analysis:** Instantly processes complex UI screenshots using Gemma's advanced vision capabilities.
- **Enterprise Cyber-Security UI:** Built with custom dark-mode aesthetics, glowing accents, and high-contrast security indicators.
- **Actionable Consumer Protection:** Translates complex legal frameworks into everyday language.
- **Zero-Friction Deployment:** Hosted securely on Streamlit Cloud with dynamic API key injection.
